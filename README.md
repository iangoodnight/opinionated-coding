# Opininated Coding

> There's more than one way to do it, but rather than spending time
> debating what color to paint the bike shed, let's just pick a color
> and get on with it. Opinions are my own and delivered without
> reservation. If you don't like it, feel free to ignore it.

**Status:** It's early days still. Don't expect complete thoughts or
even complete sentences. All opinions are bound to get revised as I
stub my toe on them in real life.

## Why?

In the time I have spent earning a living as a software engineer, I
have reviewed and contributed enough code to find myself
cantankerously insistent on not repeating the same mistakes over and
over again. The patterns captured here are both to remind myself of
the lessons learned and to arm myself when I find myself defending
said patterns to others.
